Zelda Products Right Now

A running list of Legend of Zelda hardware, amiibo, and games currently sold for Nintendo Switch and Switch 2. Prices below are typical retail as of when we last checked — tap through to each retailer for the live price and stock.

Hardware & Accessories

Nintendo Switch 2 (standard console)

~$449.99
The current-generation console, and the hardware every Switch 2-era Zelda release builds on — including whatever an Ocarina of Time limited edition turns out to be. Nintendo's collector's editions have historically been redesigned consoles rather than separate add-ons (see the Tears of the Kingdom OLED below), so if an Ocarina bundle happens the way past ones have, it'll likely be this same console reskinned with a matching dock and Pro Controller rather than new internals. Worth knowing the base price so you can gauge how much of a premium any special edition carries once it's announced.

Switch OLED — Tears of the Kingdom Edition

~$359.99
Released alongside Tears of the Kingdom in 2023, this remains the closest real-world precedent for what a Switch-generation Zelda special edition looks like: a gold-and-white Hylian Crest dock, matching Joy-Con, and a printed system menu theme, all built on the standard OLED hardware rather than anything mechanically different. It sold out at most retailers within the first weeks of launch and has mostly moved to resale since — original stock at MSRP is rare at this point, so most listings you'll find now run above the original price. Useful as a pricing and demand benchmark for how fast a future Ocarina edition might move.

Zelda TOTK Pro Controller & Case

~$74.99 / ~$24.99
Sold as a companion accessory to the OLED bundle above rather than packed in with it — Nintendo has generally sold the controller and console separately even when their designs are matched. That's a pattern worth watching for: past collector's-edition consoles haven't reliably come with a matching controller in the box, so if you want the full themed set for an Ocarina release, budget for the controller as a separate purchase.

Current amiibo

TOTK amiibo — Riju, Sidon, Yunobo, Tulin

~$16–$30 each
The four-figure wave tied to Tears of the Kingdom's playable Sage companions, released in step with the Switch 2 launch window. Unlike older amiibo lines, this set doesn't unlock cosmetic-only extras — scanning each figure grants in-game abilities tied to that character's Sage power, which is part of why prices on this wave haven't settled the way older amiibo eventually do. Retailer stock is inconsistent enough that noticeable price swings between sellers on the same day aren't unusual; if you're chasing a specific figure rather than the full set, it's worth checking back rather than assuming the first listing you find is the going rate.
Nintendo Store Best Buy Amazon

Mineru's Construct amiibo

~$99.99
A later-wave release built at a noticeably larger scale than the standard figures above, reflecting Mineru's role as a mechanical Construct piloted from within rather than a standard humanoid character. Priced roughly 4x the standard wave, in line with Nintendo's past pattern of charging a premium for oversized or mechanically-distinct amiibo (comparable to how Guardian and other large-format amiibo have been priced in previous Zelda lines). Availability has been the more limiting factor than price — it's sold directly through Nintendo's own store more often than through third-party retailers.

Games

Breath of the Wild — Switch 2 Edition

Upgrade pack pricing varies
An enhanced version of the original 2017 game built to take advantage of Switch 2's hardware — better resolution and frame rate rather than new content. If you already own the original Switch version, Nintendo's upgrade path is typically cheaper than buying the Switch 2 edition outright, so it's worth checking your eShop library for an upgrade offer before purchasing fresh. This re-release pattern (base game plus a paid Switch 2 upgrade rather than a full remaster sold separately) is the same one Nintendo is expected to use for any future re-releases tied to the confirmed Ocarina of Time remake.
Nintendo eShop

Tears of the Kingdom — Switch 2 Edition

Upgrade pack pricing varies
The Switch 2 enhancement pass for 2023's direct sequel to Breath of the Wild, following the same upgrade-pack model rather than a standalone re-release. Since this and the BOTW upgrade above are the two existing examples of how Nintendo handles enhanced Switch 2 versions of older titles, they're the best real-world comparison for what pricing and format the confirmed Ocarina of Time remake might follow on Switch 2.
Nintendo eShop
