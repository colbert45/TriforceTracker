Every Rumor About the Switch 2 Ocarina Hardware, Fact-Checked

Published August 21, 2026 · Updated August 22, 2026 · Ocarina Hardware Watch

We keep getting versions of the same questions, so here's a single reference post pulling together every claim we've seen floating around, sorted by how solid the evidence actually is. We'll update this as things change — treat it as a living document, not a one-time snapshot.

ClaimStatus
An Ocarina of Time remake is in developmentConfirmed. Officially announced at Nintendo's June 9, 2026 Direct, targeting a 2026 window with no specific date given. Source: Nintendo's own news page.
A black/dark-green Switch 2 console with gold Triforce detailing existsLeaked. Box art surfaced August 11, 2026, corroborated by Nintendo Life, Kotaku, TechRadar, 9to5Toys, and NintendoReporters, plus journalist Necro Felipe, who says he's seen the products firsthand. Not officially confirmed.
A matching Pro Controller with a Hylian Crest design existsLeaked, same sourcing as the console above. Not officially confirmed.
The hardware is specifically "Ocarina of Time" brandedDisputed. Leak framing shifted toward general "Zelda 40th anniversary" branding in late July 2026, per leaker Shpeshal Nick, corroborated by multiple outlets.
The remake's ESRB rating hints at a redesigned Great FairySpeculation. The E10+ rating (revealed July 29, 2026) drops the "Suggestive Themes" descriptor the 2011 3DS remake carried; several outlets have floated the connection, but Nintendo hasn't confirmed any character redesign.
The hardware and the remake will launch togetherSpeculation. Historically, Zelda special-edition hardware has often launched slightly ahead of its associated game, not simultaneously — see our special-editions timing post.
Trademark filings prove the hardware is comingWeak signal, not proof. Nintendo's "Ocarina of Time" trademark dates to 2007 and has simply been swept into routine renewal batches since — most recently in 2020, alongside roughly 38 unrelated older titles. See our post on the Direct vs. the leak.
Pricing for the special-edition hardwareNot announced anywhere we've seen. Any specific number circulating should be treated as unverified.
An exact release date for the console, controller, or remakeNot announced. Nintendo has given a 2026 window for the remake and nothing more specific for either it or the hardware.

Why the branding question matters more than it looks

The gap between "Ocarina of Time hardware" and "Zelda 40th anniversary hardware" isn't just semantics — it changes what timing logic even applies. We went deep on this distinction, and on what history suggests about launch timing generally, in our piece on past Zelda special editions.

How we're sourcing this

We're not a news outlet and we don't have independent sourcing of our own — everything above is our attempt to organize public reporting and leaks from games press, not original reporting. If you're seeing a claim about this hardware that isn't listed here, it's either something we haven't come across yet or something we didn't find corroborated closely enough to include. Feel free to point us at it — see our mailbag post for how we handle reader questions.
