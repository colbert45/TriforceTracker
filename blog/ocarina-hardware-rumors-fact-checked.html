Every Rumor About the Switch 2 Ocarina Hardware, Fact-Checked

Published August 21, 2026 · Ocarina Hardware Watch

We keep getting versions of the same questions, so here's a single reference post pulling together every claim we've seen floating around, sorted by how solid the evidence actually is. We'll update this as things change — treat it as a living document, not a one-time snapshot.

ClaimStatus
An Ocarina of Time remake is in developmentConfirmed by Nintendo, targeting a 2026 window with no specific date given.
A black-and-gold special-edition Switch 2 dock with Triforce detailing existsLeaked, not officially confirmed.

The hardware is specifically "Ocarina of Time" brandedUnclear — some sources describe it as general Zelda 40th-anniversary branding instead, not tied to any one game.
The hardware and the remake will launch togetherSpeculation. Historically, Zelda special-edition hardware has often launched slightly ahead of its associated game, not simultaneously.
Pricing for the special-edition hardwareNot announced anywhere we've seen. Any specific number circulating should be treated as unverified.
An exact release date for the console or the remakeNot announced. Nintendo has given a 2026 window for the remake and nothing more specific for either it or the hardware.

Why the branding question matters more than it looks

The gap between "Ocarina of Time hardware" and "Zelda 40th anniversary hardware" isn't just semantics — it changes what timing logic even applies. We went deep on this distinction, and on what history suggests about launch timing generally, in our piece on past Zelda special editions.

How we're sourcing this

We're not a news outlet and we don't have independent sourcing of our own — everything above is our attempt to organize public reporting and leaks from games press, not original reporting. If you're seeing a claim about this hardware that isn't listed here, it's either something we haven't come across yet or something we didn't find corroborated closely enough to include.
