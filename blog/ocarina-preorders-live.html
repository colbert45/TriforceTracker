Pre-Orders Are Live for the Zelda 40th Anniversary Switch 2

Published September 8, 2026 · Ocarina Hardware Watch

Update, September 8, 2026: the console pre-order window was short. It sold out at every retailer we tracked in about four hours — resellers are already listing it on eBay for $750–$900+. The pricing details below are still accurate; treat the "up for pre-order" language as describing that first window, not current availability.

Earlier today we wrote about the gap: Ocarina of Time had a release date, the anniversary hardware had a release date, and only the game was actually up for pre-order. That gap didn't last the day. Hours after the Zelda 40th Anniversary Direct wrapped (see our full breakdown of the reveal if you missed it), Nintendo opened pre-orders for the hardware too.

The price landed right where guesses put it

The Zelda 40th Anniversary Switch 2 — console, dock, and Joy-Con — went up for pre-order at $519.99, direct from Nintendo and at Best Buy. That's a $20 premium over the standard $499.99 Switch 2, close to the Tears of the Kingdom OLED's modest $10 premium back in 2023, and right in the $499.99–$529.99 range most pre-Direct guesses landed on. No pricing surprise here — the number matched expectations about as closely as a Nintendo limited edition ever does. The surprise was how fast it moved.

Worth being precise about what that $519.99 actually buys: it's the console, dock, and Joy-Con only. The matching Pro Controller and carrying case, both shown at the same Direct, are sold separately and are not up for pre-order yet — no listing, date, or price for either has surfaced anywhere as of this writing. If you want the full themed set, budget for two more purchases later, not one box today.

The game's pricing held up

The Ocarina of Time remake itself is also up for pre-order, at $59.99 digital / $69.99 physical — confirming the same figures that leaked via a since-pulled Nintendo Australia eShop listing before the Direct even happened. Unlike the console, this one landed right where the leak said it would. It launches November 5, 2026, one week after the hardware.

Where things stand now

The game is still up on the Nintendo eShop, with retailers like Best Buy also carrying it — that one hasn't sold out. The console is a different story: both Nintendo's own store and Best Buy are showing it sold out as of this writing, with no restock window announced. We've got current status and links on our products page, and we'll update it the moment a restock or second wave opens.

For what it's worth: one of us already pulled the trigger before it sold out — console from Best Buy, game from Nintendo, in that order. Neither purchase felt like a rumor anymore by the time the confirmation email landed.

We'll post again the moment the console restocks, or the Pro Controller and case show up for pre-order — those are the open questions left in this whole saga.
