FAQ

Questions People Ask

The short answers to what we get asked most, in one place.

Is this an official Nintendo site?

No. Ocarina Hardware Watch is an independent fan project run by two individual fans. We're not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Nintendo Co., Ltd. in any way. All game titles, hardware names, and related trademarks belong to Nintendo.

Is the Ocarina of Time remake actually confirmed?

Yes — that part is settled. Nintendo officially announced the remake during a June 9, 2026 Nintendo Direct, confirming a 2026 release window with no specific date. See our full breakdown for the timeline.

Is the special-edition hardware confirmed too?

No, not yet. The console and Pro Controller are still at the "leaked" stage — box art surfaced on August 11, 2026, corroborated by several outlets, but Nintendo hasn't confirmed the hardware, its price, or any date. Check the live tracker for the current status.

When is it releasing?

Nobody outside Nintendo actually knows yet. Some rumors point to a November 12, 2026 window, but that's speculation based on leaked retail listings, not an official date. We'll update the tracker the moment anything firms up.

Can I buy the hardware or products from you?

No — we don't sell anything ourselves. Our product directory links out to retailers like Nintendo's own store, Best Buy, and Amazon for anything currently available.

Where do you get your news from?

The tracker pulls public news headlines and links to their original sources — we don't reproduce full articles. Leaked images on our Photos page are embedded directly from the original poster's X account.

I spotted news you missed — can I tell you?

Please do. Reach us through the contact info on our About page.
