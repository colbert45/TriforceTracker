What's confirmed so far

Nintendo officially announced the Ocarina of Time remake during a June 9, 2026 Nintendo Direct, confirming a 2026 release window with no specific date — that part is a settled fact, straight from Nintendo. The special-edition hardware this site tracks is a separate, still-unconfirmed story: box art for a matching Switch 2 console and Pro Controller began circulating on August 11, 2026, corroborated by several outlets and a journalist claiming firsthand contact with the products. Nintendo has not confirmed the hardware, its price, or any date.

Why we're tracking this specific hardware

Nintendo has a long, fairly consistent pattern of pairing a major Zelda release with special-edition hardware — the gold Ocarina of Time-branded Game Boy Advance SP, the Skyward Sword and Breath of the Wild Wii U/Switch bundles, and most recently the Tears of the Kingdom OLED console in 2023. None of those were ever pre-announced far in advance; they typically leaked in the weeks or months before a reveal, then got folded into a Direct alongside the game itself. That pattern is exactly why an unconfirmed leak is worth tracking closely rather than dismissing: it's the stage every prior release in this line has actually gone through before becoming official. We go deeper on that pattern in our history of Zelda special editions.

What makes this case slightly murkier than past ones is the branding ambiguity: early coverage described the leaked hardware as tied specifically to Ocarina of Time, but framing in more recent reporting has drifted toward calling it general "40th Anniversary" hardware instead, which could mean it ships alongside the remake without being exclusively themed around it. We track that distinction because it changes what "released" would even mean for the purposes of this tracker's stage above — see our breakdown of the Direct vs. the leak for the full reasoning.
