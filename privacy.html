Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated August 19, 2026

Ocarina Hardware Watch ("we," "this site") is a small, independently run fan blog. This page explains what data is and isn't collected when you visit.

What we collect directly

We don't require an account, and we don't run our own analytics or tracking scripts. We don't collect names, emails, or personal information unless you choose to email us directly.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub, as our hosting provider, may automatically log standard technical data (such as IP address and browser type) as part of serving the site. That logging is governed by GitHub's own privacy statement, not by us directly.

Embedded content from other sites

Some pages embed content hosted by other companies, which may set their own cookies or collect data according to their own privacy policies, independent of us:

  • X (Twitter) timelines — embedded posts on our tracker page are loaded via X's official widget, governed by X's privacy policy
  • News headlines — our "Check now" feature retrieves public headlines through a third-party feed service; no personal data is sent as part of that request
  • Google Fonts — this site loads typefaces from Google Fonts, which may log basic request data per Google's privacy policy

Advertising

We intend to display ads through Google AdSense to help support the cost of running this site. When ads are active, Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can learn more about how Google uses this data, and manage your ad preferences, at Google's Ads Policy page and Google Ads Settings.

Your choices

Most browsers let you block or delete cookies through their settings. Doing so may affect how embedded content (like the X timeline) displays, but won't prevent you from reading the site itself.

Changes to this policy

We may update this page as the site changes, particularly once advertising goes live. We'll update the "last updated" date above when we do.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at hello@triforcetracker.com.
